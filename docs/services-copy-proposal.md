## Six services, six concrete outcomes

### Web Development

**A website that explains your business. A web app that gets work done.**

We design and build marketing websites, customer portals, and web application interfaces that make your offer clear and your users' next step easy.

**When to bring us in:** You are launching a business, replacing an outdated website, or turning a product idea into a usable web experience.

**What we deliver**

- Page structure and content organized around your audience and offer.
- Responsive interface designs for desktop and mobile.
- Implemented pages, reusable components, and the agreed user journeys.
- Technical SEO foundations and accessibility and performance checks.
- Source code, setup instructions, test results, and release guidance.

**What you leave with:** A working website or web interface, reviewed against the agreed scope, with the materials your team needs to maintain it.

**Scope:** Backend services, payments, CMS integrations, hosting setup, and ongoing content support are agreed separately when needed.

**Delivery:** Understand → Design → Build → Test → Hand over

CTA: **Discuss your web project** → `/contact`

### Mobile App Development

**Bring your service into your customers' hands.**

We turn a mobile product idea into an app built around the actions your users need most, from the first screen through the core workflow.

**When to bring us in:** You need a first mobile release, a better experience in an existing app, or a mobile companion to your web product.

**What we deliver**

- A focused release scope and clear user journeys.
- Navigation, screen designs, and an interactive prototype.
- An implemented app for the platform or platforms agreed for the project.
- Device and workflow testing, including loading, error, and empty states.
- Source code, build instructions, release checklist, and handover documentation.

**What you leave with:** A tested app build covering the agreed journeys, plus the source and release materials needed for the next step.

**Scope:** iOS, Android, or cross-platform delivery, backend work, device integrations, and store submission support are confirmed before development. Store approval remains subject to the platform's review.

**Delivery:** Define → Prototype → Build → Test → Prepare release

CTA: **Discuss your mobile app** → `/contact`

### AI Agent Development

**Turn repeatable work into an AI-assisted workflow.**

We build agents that work with your approved tools and information to complete defined tasks, with clear limits on what they can do and when they need your team's approval.

**When to bring us in:** Your team repeatedly gathers information, prepares outputs, or moves work between systems, and you want to automate a specific part of that process.

**What we deliver**

- A workflow map defining inputs, actions, outputs, and approval steps.
- Agent implementation with the agreed tool and system integrations.
- Access rules and human review for sensitive actions.
- Evaluation scenarios for successful tasks, incorrect outputs, and tool failures.
- Run logs, operating instructions, and a handover of known limitations.

**What you leave with:** A working agent for the agreed task, with reviewed evaluation results and a clear way for your team to supervise it.

**Scope:** We agree on data access, actions, model providers, and usage costs before implementation. For a complete user-facing AI experience, choose AI Product Engineering.

**Delivery:** Map the task → Connect tools → Build → Evaluate → Hand over

CTA: **Discuss your workflow** → `/contact`

### AI Product Engineering

**Make AI a useful part of your product.**

We design and build AI features that fit a real user journey, connecting the interface, model, and approved data with clear behavior when an answer is missing or wrong.

**When to bring us in:** You have an AI idea or prototype and need to turn it into a feature people can use, understand, and give feedback on.

**What we deliver**

- A defined use case and acceptance criteria for the first release.
- Interface and interaction designs, including feedback and fallback paths.
- An implemented AI feature with the agreed model and data integrations.
- Quality evaluations and latency and usage-cost checks for agreed scenarios.
- Release documentation, operating guidance, and documented limitations.

**What you leave with:** An integrated AI feature tested against the agreed use case, with the evidence and instructions needed to assess release readiness.

**Scope:** Data preparation, provider charges, hosting, and ongoing model evaluation are agreed for each project. For an agent that takes actions across tools, choose AI Agent Development.

**Delivery:** Define value → Design → Integrate → Evaluate → Prepare release

CTA: **Discuss your AI product** → `/contact`

### Platform Modernization

**Improve the system your business already depends on.**

We assess your existing platform and carry out agreed improvements in manageable stages, with checks and a rollback approach for each release.

**When to bring us in:** An existing application is difficult to change, integrations are fragile, or an aging platform is holding back the next release.

**What we deliver**

- A map of the current system and its important dependencies.
- Prioritized changes tied to your operational and product needs.
- A phased modernization plan with clear acceptance criteria.
- Implemented improvements for the agreed phase, with migration work where scoped.
- Validation results, rollback instructions, and updated operating documentation.

**What you leave with:** A completed modernization phase, reviewed against its acceptance criteria, and a documented path for the remaining work.

**Scope:** Assessment can be purchased separately. Implementation, data migration, deployment windows, and production access require explicit agreement for each phase.

**Delivery:** Assess → Prioritize → Implement → Validate → Hand over

CTA: **Discuss your platform** → `/contact`

### Architecture & Governance

**Make the next technical decision with a clear path forward.**

We review your architecture and delivery practices, identify the decisions that matter, and turn them into a practical roadmap your team can follow.

**When to bring us in:** You need an independent review before a major build, clearer engineering standards, or a consistent way to approve and verify changes.

**What we deliver**

- Architecture and dependency review with prioritized findings.
- Technical options and trade-offs linked to your requirements.
- A decision register with decision ownership and next actions.
- Practical review, approval, and verification checklists.
- An implementation roadmap and a walkthrough with your team.

**What you leave with:** A documented technical direction, clear responsibilities, and an actionable plan for delivery.

**Scope:** This is an advisory engagement. Implementation can be scoped as a follow-on project. Reviews do not constitute legal advice, certification, or a compliance audit.

**Delivery:** Inspect → Compare options → Decide → Document → Walk through

CTA: **Discuss your technical challenge** → `/contact`

## What completion looks like

### Clear scope. Visible progress. A practical handover.

Before work begins, we agree on the deliverables, acceptance criteria, responsibilities, and what is outside the project. During delivery, you review working increments at agreed milestones. At handover, you receive the completed work, the relevant source and documentation, and a record of checks and any accepted limitations.

Launch and ongoing support are agreed as part of the project scope.
